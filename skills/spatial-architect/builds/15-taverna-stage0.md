# Stage 0 - Cycladic taverna, 16:40, arch onto the Aegean

**Function (one sentence).** A shaded room whose entire purpose is the bright
thing outside it - people come in out of the sun in order to sit and look at
the sea.

That sentence is the correction. The last two builds were sealed boxes lit by
one small warm source, and the skill exists for *interiors, often with a view
outside*. Here the interior is the frame and the light is the subject.

**Light archetype (L10): contre-jour + sun shapes.** A near-white opening with
the room reading against it, plus hard-edged sun figures raked across the floor
and up the inner wall. Deliberately NOT the hearth.

**Why 16:40 and not noon.** Sun elevation at Aegean noon is ~72 deg, so light
through a 2.6m arch reaches **0.84m** into the room - a lick at the threshold.
At 16:40 the sun is ~35 deg up and the same arch throws light **3.71m** deep,
across the floor and onto the back wall. The hour was chosen by the arithmetic,
not by mood.

**Envelope.** ROOM: 5.40w x 6.00d x 3.00h (vaulted, thick rubble walls)
- SPAN width: bench=0.55 tables=3.60 counter=1.25 = 5.40 OK
- SPAN depth: arch-wall=0.45 dining=4.05 counter-run=1.50 = 6.00 OK

**Dominant by volume - the architecture, not an object.**
| item | real w x h (m) | vs the woman (1.70m) |
|---|---|---|
| arch opening | 2.40 x 2.60 | 1.5x her height |
| wall thickness (reveal) | 0.45 | deep enough to cast its own shadow band |
| taverna table | 0.80 x 0.75 | thigh height |
| rush chair | 0.45 x 0.88 | - |
| counter | 1.60 x 1.05 | chest height |
| woman, standing | 0.50 x 1.70 | the yardstick |

**Scene-specific constraints:**
1. Limewash is laid thick over rubble: **no sharp arris anywhere**. Every corner
   is rounded, every wall slightly bellied, every edge softened.
2. Walls are 0.45m thick, so the arch has a deep reveal - the light shape on the
   floor is offset inward from the opening, and the reveal's own shadow reads as
   a hard band on the jamb.
3. **The sea horizon IS the horizon line.** It sits at y_h exactly, because both
   are the camera's eye level. Getting this wrong is instantly visible.
4. Limewash albedo is ~0.90. In direct sun it clips to white - which is where
   L9's near-white territory comes from, honestly rather than by fiat.
5. Shade here is lit by sky and sea, not by the sun, so it is frankly blue - the
   two illuminants are 4300K and 9500K and the picture must show both whites.
6. Cobalt joinery is the identity accent and appears only on joinery.

**Camera.** 1.75m standing - the tallest in the corpus. k=288, datum 820, so
R = 288*1.75 = 504 and the horizon is y=316 (F1). VP (700,316).

**What the frame cuts.** A chair back and table corner cropped bottom-left; the
counter runs out of frame right; bougainvillea crops the top of the arch.
